Verify the deployed function bodies: no cfg(kani), default features.

Report each property under its label, proved or refuted with a concrete counterexample. Do not weaken a contract silently; say so if you had to add a precondition, and list every library function whose documented behaviour a proof assumes instead of covering by its body. Do not work on any other function in this task, and do not resume the three prepare_counterparty_* functions.
