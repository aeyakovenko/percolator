What is new. Until now the targets were the pure kernels (associated functions of `V16Core`) and a few small entry points. The kernels are also covered by bounded model checking. This task is about the execution of a trade, which no model-checking harness reaches at all: no harness calls `execute_trade_with_fee_loss_stale_scoped_not_atomic` or `execute_batch_with_fee_loss_stale_scoped_not_atomic`, and none reaches the private methods below them. So today the only evidence for this code is tests and fuzzing. It is a pilot: three methods, and I want to learn how such proofs have to be cut and what they cost.

How to cut it. Prove each method from its own body and from contracts of the methods it calls. The contracts I believe hold are listed below as TC1 to TC9. In Parts 1 to 3 use them as explicit hypotheses instead of going through the callee's body, and say in each result which of them it used. Part 4 then discharges them, shallowest first. That keeps every proof about one function body. If you find that a contract is false, or too weak for a property that needs it, that is the most useful result of this task: report it (a counterexample with the starting state and inputs if you have one), state the contract that does hold, and go on with that one.

Do the parts in order, and inside a part the properties in order. Record each finished property before starting the next, so that finished results are kept if the task stops early. This task has a fixed size: if a property does not yield after a serious attempt, record it as unresolved with the obstacle in one or two sentences and go on to the next one; do not spend the rest of the task on it.

Notation. `m` is the market view (header `m.header`, slots `m.markets`); `L` and `S` are the two portfolio accounts passed in (`long_account`, `short_account`), each with its account record `.header`. Primed values are after the call. Properties are for all inputs and all starting states that the types allow, about calls that return `Ok`, unless they say otherwise. "Writes" means the value after the call can differ from the value before. A certificate is the account's `health_cert`. The deployed build has no `audit-scan` feature, so `validate_shape_audit_scan` and `validate_account_audit_scan` are the deployed (empty) versions.

Part 1. `finish_trade_checks_not_atomic(long_account, short_account, locked, risk_increasing, long_requires_initial_margin, short_requires_initial_margin, long_has_source_claims, short_has_source_claims) -> Ok(())`. It is the margin gate at the end of every trade.

- TF.margin: for each of the two accounts whose `*_requires_initial_margin` argument is true, at the return its certificate is valid, `certified_equity >= 0` and `certified_equity >= certified_initial_req`. (No trade that needed initial margin from an account ends with that account below it.) Uses the contract of `kernel_initial_margin_gate` and TC9.
- TF.locked: if `locked` is true, then for each account whose `*_requires_initial_margin` argument is true, at the return `capital + min(pnl, 0) - |fee_credits| >= certified_initial_req` and that left side is not negative, with capital, pnl and fee_credits the account's fields at the return. Uses the contract of `kernel_locked_margin_gate`.
- TF.frame: if `risk_increasing` is false or `locked` is true, the call writes nothing at all. In every case the call writes no field of an account other than capital, health_cert and source_domains, and no header field other than backing_provider_earnings_total, c_tot, risk_epoch, source_claim_bound_total_num, source_fresh_backing_total_num and source_insurance_credit_reserved_total_atoms. Uses TC9.
- TF.order, a question. The long account's lien is created and its certificate renewed (inside TC9's function) before the short account's lien is created, and the long account's margin is then checked against its stored certificate. Say whether creating the short account's lien can change anything the long account's certificate was computed from (so that a fresh certification of the long account would give a lower `certified_equity` or a higher `certified_initial_req`). A precise statement of what the certificate depends on and what the lien creation writes is the result I want; do not go beyond the two functions' own bodies and TC9 unless it is cheap.

Part 2. `apply_trade_after_refresh_not_atomic(long_account, short_account, request, position_lookups, recertify_after_fill, is_final_batch_fill, long_attributable_asset_before_refresh, short_attributable_asset_before_refresh) -> Ok(outcome)`. It applies one fill to two accounts that have just been settled and certified. Write `fee = checked_fee_bps(trade_fee_notional_ceil(|request.size_q|, request.exec_price), request.fee_bps)`.

- TA.fee: outcome.fee_a <= fee and outcome.fee_b <= fee; more exactly outcome.fee_a == 0 if fee == 0 or L.header.pnl < 0, else min(fee, L.header.capital), and the same for fee_b with S. outcome.notional == floor(|request.size_q| * request.exec_price / POS_SCALE). Uses TC1.
- TA.conservation: m'.header.vault == m.header.vault; m'.header.c_tot == m.header.c_tot - outcome.fee_a - outcome.fee_b; m'.header.insurance == m.header.insurance + outcome.fee_a + outcome.fee_b; L'.header.capital == L.header.capital - outcome.fee_a and S'.header.capital == S.header.capital - outcome.fee_b; L'.header.pnl == L.header.pnl and S'.header.pnl == S.header.pnl. (Applying a fill moves no token value and no PnL; the only value that moves is the fee, from each account's capital to insurance.) Uses TC1 to TC7.
- TA.positions: for each account, with `lk` its lookup returned by the preflight (TC2: the lookup passed in): after the call the account's signed raw position on asset request.asset_index is lk.next_q (an absent or inactive leg, or an active leg with raw basis 0, counts as 0), and its legs on every other asset are what they were. Uses TC2 and TC5.
- TA.gate: the side that the fill grew does not end above the side cap: state exactly what `kernel_trade_side_oi_cap_gate` guarantees about the asset's `oi_eff_long_q` and `oi_eff_short_q` before and after the two position changes, using its contract.
- TA.frame: the call writes no header field other than c_tot, insurance, resolved_payout_blocker_count and risk_epoch; no account field other than active_bitmap, capital, close_progress, health_cert, legs, liquidation_lock, residual_received_atoms_total and residual_spent_principal_atoms_total; and no market slot other than the slot of asset request.asset_index. Uses TC1 to TC7.
- TA.nopanic: no execution of the function's own body panics outside the callees named in TC1 to TC8 (it may return Err). List the arithmetic sites you checked.

Part 3. `execute_batch_with_fee_loss_stale_scoped_not_atomic(long_account, short_account, requests)`, the public entry point. Treat its callee `execute_batch_with_fee_after_tail_validation_not_atomic` as an opaque call that can write any field of the market and of the two accounts and return Ok or Err.

- TB.flag: let f0 be m.header.loss_stale_active at entry, and let `ignore` be the value the local `ignore_unrelated_loss_stale` has when the opaque call is made. If `ignore` is true, the opaque call starts with m.header.loss_stale_active == 0, and at every return of the entry point after that call, Ok or Err, m.header.loss_stale_active == f0. If `ignore` is false the entry point itself never writes the flag. (The scoped suspension of the flag never leaks, in either direction, on any path.) Part 4. The contracts. Discharge them in this order; TC5 and TC9 are deep and are the subject of the next task, so attempt them only if everything else is finished.

- TC1 `charge_account_fee_current_not_atomic(account, requested_fee) -> Ok(charged)`: charged == 0 if requested_fee == 0 or account.header.pnl < 0, else charged == min(requested_fee, account.header.capital). If charged == 0 nothing is written. Otherwise exactly these are written: account.header.capital (lowered by charged), m.header.c_tot (lowered by charged), m.header.insurance (raised by charged), account.header.health_cert.valid (set to 0). m.header.vault is not written. This one has no deep callee: prove it from its body, including that no execution panics.
- TC2 `validate_trade_position_preflight(long_account, short_account, request, long_lookup, short_lookup) -> Ok(p)` (takes `&self`): writes nothing; p.long_lookup == long_lookup and p.short_lookup == short_lookup; p.long_old_abs_q == |long_lookup.current_effective_q|, p.long_new_abs_q == |long_lookup.next_effective_q|, and the same for short; p.risk_increasing is true exactly when |next_effective_q| > |current_effective_q| for at least one of the two lookups.
- TC3 `require_asset_risk_change_allowed(asset_index, risk_increasing)` (takes `&self`): writes nothing; with risk_increasing true it returns Ok only if the asset's `a_long` and `a_short` both equal ADL_ONE.
- TC6 `transfer_trade_residual_reward_credit(...)` (takes `&self`): writes nothing in the market, and in the two accounts only residual_received_atoms_total and residual_spent_principal_atoms_total.
- TC7 `recertify_account_after_trade_delta(account, ...)`: writes nothing in the market header and in the account only health_cert.
- TC8 `kernel_trade_side_oi_cap_gate`: its contract in aristotle/kernel_contracts_spec.md if it is listed there; otherwise its exact return condition from its body.
- TC4 `begin_terminal_trade_residual_if_needed(account, ...)`: writes no header field other than resolved_payout_blocker_count and no account field other than close_progress; writes nothing at all when `is_final_batch_fill` is false, when the lookup's current_q is 0 or its next_q is not 0, or when account.header.pnl >= 0.
- TC5 `apply_current_position_delta_with_lookup(account, asset_index, delta_q, lookup)`: when it returns Ok with delta_q != 0, the account's signed raw position on asset_index is lookup.next_q afterwards (as counted in TA.positions) and its legs on other assets are unchanged; it writes no header field other than resolved_payout_blocker_count and risk_epoch, no account field other than active_bitmap, health_cert, legs and liquidation_lock, and no market slot other than the slot of asset_index. In particular it writes none of capital, pnl, c_tot, insurance, vault.
- TC9 `create_initial_margin_source_lien_if_needed(account)`: when it returns Ok, the account's certificate is valid at the return; it writes no account field other than capital, health_cert and source_domains, and no header field other than backing_provider_earnings_total, c_tot, risk_epoch, source_claim_bound_total_num, source_fresh_backing_total_num and source_insurance_credit_reserved_total_atoms. It never writes the other account (it is not passed in).
