# Where Kani does not reach (engine revision 08e8f7f0)

Generated by `kani_gap_map.py` from `kani_reach.py`. The Kani suite has 510 harnesses. This file
orders the prover's work: it goes first where Kani has nothing.

Three measures, from weakest to strongest evidence of a gap:

1. **No harness reaches the function through the call graph.** Certain: Kani never executes it.
   6 of the 760 deployed engine functions, 1 of the 214 that change state. Listed below.
2. **A harness reaches it, but perhaps never executes it.** The call graph says what a harness could
   call, not what it does: `full_account_refresh_not_atomic` is reached by one harness, which
   proves that a call is rejected before the refresh runs. Only Kani's coverage mode tells
   (`cargo kani --tests --features fuzz --coverage -Z source-coverage`, then `kani_cov_report.py`).
3. **A harness executes it but asserts something else.** Not measured here.

An earlier version of this file (2026-11-20) counted the methods that no proof names, and
reported 124 state-changing methods under five entry points. That count was wrong as a measure of coverage: by the call graph the same five entry
points reach only 18 state-changing methods that no harness reaches.

## What the proof suite itself records as beyond Kani

that were attempted. They are quoted here because they are the map of Kani's limits by the people
who hit them; the prover's first targets are taken from this list and from the certain gaps above.

**`tests/proofs_v16.rs` lines 18583 to 18592**

> Clean-room inductive senior-solvency proof (independent of any external PR).
> 
> validate_shape enforces the senior leg `c_tot + insurance (+ earnings) <= vault`
> and per-account `capital <= c_tot` via an O(N) loop scan, which makes
> assume(validate_shape) intractable over full-domain symbolic state. This decomposes
> the senior-solvency invariant into a loop-free predicate, assumes it over FULLY
> SYMBOLIC u128/i128 economic scalars (no <=1000 bounds), applies the bare negative-PnL
> principal-settlement transition, and proves INV(s) => INV(f(s)) plus the exact
> value-conservation delta laws. Covers fire on partial and full settlement
> (non-vacuous). No markets/legs are touched by this transition, so unwind(8) holds.

**`tests/proofs_v16.rs` lines 19304 to 19313**

> Expiry-liveness primitive (wrapper finding 2026-06-10): the resolved-close
> realize step must not strand a source-backed winner whose backing has lapsed
> (bucket still Fresh but expiry_slot <= current_slot — nothing processes
> expiry in production). Querying realizable support against a past-expiry
> bucket returns Stale. The primitive that avoids the deadlock: expiring the
> lapsed bucket forfeits its principal (fresh_reserved -> 0), drops the domain
> credit rate to zero, and makes realizable support exactly zero — so the
> realize step falls through to the junior receipt path instead of reverting.
> The full close_resolved path is Kani-intractable; the two bounded route
> bindings below pin this primitive for under-backed and fully-backed claims.

**`tests/proofs_v16.rs` lines 20182 to 20189**

> ============ GATES OF THE ENTRY POINTS NO OTHER PROOF REACHED ============
> The trade, the rebalance reduction and the Recovery routes are beyond Kani's budget once
> their bodies run (see the INTRACTABLE-TIER notes above), and until these proofs no harness
> called their entry points at all. What Kani can do for them is the gate: a call that must be
> refused is refused with the documented error, and nothing in the market or in either account
