# INV-045 (no free mark movement): the statement in pieces (engine 71076137, program f6d0bc57)

Restated 2026-11-22 after the independent read of `specs/inv045_closure_4e77481b.md` (its findings, the author's check
and the reconciliation of the writer lists are in the review notes, `review/inv045-read-2026-11-22/`). Since then the
owner reworded the charter (program e1295564) and the engine restarts the price-cap anchor when a staged target
changes (engine 71076137, batch 15). On 2026-11-23 the program added the catch-up rule (program f6d0bc57, clause
C11). The clauses below are new where marked; the rest keep their pieces.

The charter text (program `INVARIANTS.md`, e1295564): while the asset has effective open interest on either side, the
mark moves toward its target by at most `anchor × cap` per elapsed slot, where the anchor is the mark when the current
target took effect. A reported trade price is clamped by the same per-slot cap from the program's price average
(`mark_ewma_e6`), whatever the open interest. A raw target of 0 or above MAX is refused. No CPI/no-CPI, single/batch,
self-controlled, zero-fee, zero-size, or zero-price route bypasses the cap. Its text block:
`|mark_after - mark_before| * 10_000 <= anchor * configured_cap * elapsed_slots + carried_remainder`, the remainder
below 10,000 (under one price unit). Scope: with no effective open interest an accepted update may move the mark to its
target in one step; such an update accrues no funding, and its mark-to-market reaches only the #132 residue, at most
`raw_basis_sum_side / ADL_ONE` units per side with `A_side == 1`, moved as any capped path to the same price would move
it. Scope: funding is not independent of report cadence; two histories that differ only in how often the report is
renewed accrue funding that differs by at most `max_abs_funding_e9_per_slot` of the notional per slot of the gap.
Scope (program f6d0bc57): a target change that nobody pays for, an oracle authority's push or a reading or EWMA mark that
the permissionless crank brings, is applied to at most one window (`max_accrual_dt_slots`) of the slots before it
arrived; while the market is live and the asset has effective open interest, a push that changes the target of an
asset more than one window behind is refused, and the crank holds such a reading back and accrues toward the target in
force. A trade print that moves the mark is staged as before and pays the mark-movement fee for the whole move.

## Terms

- **Mark**: the engine's effective price, `AssetStateV16::effective_price`.
- **Update**: one engine call that writes the mark: a single-segment accrual (`accrue_asset_to_not_atomic`), a path
  accrual call (`accrue_asset_path_to_not_atomic`, one step per slot, at most 32 steps), or one of the writers of C1.
- **Elapsed slots**: the slots the call advances the asset's accrual clock (`slot_last`): `dt` for a single segment,
  one per step for the path.
- **Anchor**: `fund_px_last`. For the path accrual it is the mark when the stored raw target last changed: set by the
  path accrual when it is given a target different from the stored one, by the staging setter when it changes the
  target (batch 15), and to the mark when the mark reaches the target; activation, restart and the empty-asset reset set
  it with the target. A single-segment accrual books its own price, so its anchor is the mark before it (the cap of C2
  is measured from that mark).
- **Envelope**: a move of `m` over `dt` slots from anchor `a` is inside it when `m * 10_000 <= a * cap * dt + r`, `r`
  the remainder carried into the first step (`r < 10_000`).

## The anchor, measured (why batch 15)

Before batch 15 a staged target kept the anchor of an earlier target. The program stages targets after a trade that
moves an EWMA or hybrid mark, in the oracle authority's `PushAuthMark` (the reported price, unclamped) and before a
crank's single-segment accrual. Measured on the previous pins (review notes, `f1b/FINDINGS.md`): the authority
walked an exposed mark from 1,000,000 to 13,600 over 411 slots at 24 bps per slot (anchor 1,000,000). It then pushed
a target equal to the mark: risk-increasing trades are refused while the target differs from the mark, so this
unlocks them. A short opened there with 15% capital. A reversal pushed and cranked in the same slot then moved the
mark 17.6% (73 times the cap) and took the short's capital + PnL + unsettled K from 2,000 to -400. With batch 15 the
same slot moves it 0.23% and the short keeps 1,968. The program test
`v16_attack_authority_reversal_after_a_long_walk_moves_within_the_cap_of_the_mark` checks it.

## A price that arrives after idle slots (C11, program f6d0bc57)

The engine applies a path call's single target to every slot since the asset's last accrual, and a path call cannot
end before the market's current slot. Before C11 a target that arrived after idle slots was applied to all of them:
measured on program aac89b9e, an auth-mark asset at 1,000,000 (cap 24 bps per slot, window 6) sat idle for 200 slots,
the authority pushed 2,000,000, and 34 crank calls in that same slot moved the mark +48% and took a one-lot short with
15% capital to -330,000. That move is inside C3's bound (200 elapsed slots), so it is not a C3 violation; the owner
chose to bound it anyway (option (b)): the change reaches back at most one window, the move the solvency envelope
sizes maintenance for (`validate_exact_solvency_envelope`). A trade print is exempt: it pays the mark-movement fee
for the whole move (INV-046) and keeps its prospective staging (trade-then-crank equals crank-then-trade).

## What the bound is, and what it is not

The bound is the cap of the mark when the current target took effect, not of the current mark. Toward one far
target the walk is linear in that anchor, so late in a long walk one slot can move a large share of the mark (17.6%
of it at 13,600 in the walk above, on both pins). Two facts make that tolerable, and neither is proved yet:

- No position opens during a walk: a risk-increasing trade is refused while the target differs from the mark
  (engine `trade_preflight_risk_gate`).
- Margin counts the whole adverse gap between the mark and the raw target from the slot the target changes
  (`target_lag_penalty` in the maintenance and initial requirements, the risk notional and the health certificate).

These are ledger piece AC.walk_margin (open; a code reading today). Each change of target starts a new anchor at the
current mark, so movement across target changes compounds: if the target changed every slot, `n` slots could move
the mark by up to `(1 + cap)^n - 1` of it upward instead of `n * cap`. The excess over one window's linear budget
`B` is at most `e^B - 1 - B` (under 0.52 percentage points for `B = 10%`). Targets pushed exactly at the cap leave
the mark a few units of rounding short of the last one (the anchor restarts and the carry resets at each push), so
a risk-increasing trade waits one more slot.
