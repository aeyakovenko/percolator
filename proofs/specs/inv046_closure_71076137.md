# INV-046 (trade availability without unsafe mark admission): the statement in pieces (engine 71076137, program e1295564)

Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-22. Written from the source by its author; it needs an independent read against the charter
before any of its clauses is used (as INV-045's did: its first read found a safety hole, measured and fixed in batch 15).

The charter text (program `INVARIANTS.md`, e1295564): "A raw execution price cannot poison mark/oracle state or eliminate
every exit path. Unsafe raw prices may be ignored for discovery, safely clamped for risk/fees, or rejected only when
necessary, while at least one bounded risk-reducing route remains available." Required tests: zero, extreme, stale and
out-of-band prices during normal, drain, reset, recovery and close states.

## Terms

- **Raw execution price**: the `exec_price` an instruction carries (single and batch, no-CPI and CPI trade routes, and
  every leg of a batch). Program `handle_trade_nocpi_zero_copy` (8533), `handle_batch_trade_nocpi` (8822),
  `handle_trade_nocpi` (9255), `handle_trade_cpi` (9827), `handle_batch_trade_cpi` (10230).
- **Mark/oracle state**: the engine's effective price and raw target, and the program's oracle profile: the price average
  `mark_ewma_e6` and its slot, the staged target `oracle_target_price_e6`, the funding mark and its pending value, the
  effective-price provenance.
- **Risk-reducing route**: a position change that moves an account's position on an asset toward zero on the same side
  (or closes it), by trade, liquidation, or terminal close.

| C3 clamped for discovery | for a trade-driven asset the accepted price is `clamp_toward_engine_dt(mark_ewma_e6, raw, cap, min(now - mark_ewma_last_slot, max_accrual_dt_slots))`, and the post-trade price average is the quote clamped from the old average by the move both sides' externality fee supports (`collected_fee_supported_mark`, 6175): `min(fee_a, fee_b)` beyond the base fee, times 2, times 10,000, over the externality notional | AC.clamp (proved, k4) for the clamp; N.paid: Kani on `collected_fee_supported_mark` (the average moves by at most the supported bps from the old average, toward the quote, never past it) |

| C5 no poisoning | the only writes of mark/oracle state a trade makes are: the average and its slot (C3), the provenance flag, the funding-mark transition record, and the staged target set to the new average; the engine's effective price is not written by a trade (INV-045 C1) | N.trade_writes: the program-side write scan of the trade handlers (like AC.writers); INV-045's AC.writers for the engine |
| C6 a route remains | in every state of the market in Live mode, an account with a position has a public risk-reducing route that the price checks do not block: the risk gate refuses a target-lagging or loss-stale asset only for risk-increasing trades (`trade_preflight_risk_gate`, engine 25922), and a pending domain-loss barrier blocks a position change only when it is not a same-side reduction or the flattening of an obligation (`pending_domain_loss_barrier_blocks_position_change`, engine 26315) | N.exit_gates: Kani on the two gates (a reduction is never refused by them); the existence of the route in every reachable state is a liveness piece (phase 6, with INV-057/073/078) |
| C7 bounded | the route of C6 completes within the compute limit at the maximum shape | Y.compute (measured by the program's max-shape tests) |
